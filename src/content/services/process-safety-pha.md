## Process hazard analysis consulting

Facilities that handle highly hazardous chemicals above OSHA threshold quantities must manage process safety under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.119, and many are also subject to EPA's Risk Management Program under 40 CFR 68. Process hazard analysis consulting helps those facilities identify how a process can fail, what safeguards are in place, and where additional protection is needed.

A useful PHA is more than a compliance document. It should reflect the process as it actually operates, draw on people who know the equipment, and produce recommendations specific enough to act on and track to closure. The mechanical integrity program then has to keep the equipment those safeguards depend on in reliable condition.
